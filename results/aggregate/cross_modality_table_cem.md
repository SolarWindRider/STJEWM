**Why this is the v0.7.15 final cross-modality table:**
The earlier `cross_modality_table.md` (random policy pixel) showed
cos_dist ~ 1.0-1.25 uniformly for all models — vacuous because no model
could actually reach the goal with random actions. CEM planning on
pixel should expose the same family partition as state eval.

  representational content is preserved under modality change.

  residual error — likely due to the frozen ViT-Tiny's coarse representation.
